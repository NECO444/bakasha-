THE QUICK VERSION

How to play

Each player starts with 4 cards. The opening card is never an Ace, 2, 8, Jack, or Joker. On a normal turn, match the active suit or rank, or play a Joker. If you have no legal play, draw 1. Penalties and 8/Jack chains have special rules below.

  • AAce — Normally, its suit must match the active suit. On an Ace, any Ace may follow: agree to keep the active suit, or object and choose a different suit.
  • 2Two — Normally gives the next player a 2-card penalty. They may play another 2 to cancel the penalty, or draw 2 cards. Against a Joker penalty, a 2 reduces it by 2 instead.
  • ✳Joker — Gives the next player a 4-card penalty. While it is pending, a 2 reduces it by 2; that player may reduce it again or draw the remainder. Another Joker cancels the remainder. If the full penalty is drawn without any 2s reducing it, or a Joker cancels it, the following player may play any card. After a 2 reduces the penalty, play follows the last 2’s suit.
  • 8/JEight / Jack — Chain 8s that match the previous card by suit or rank. An odd number of 8s reverses direction when the chain ends; an even number does not. On a normal turn, a Jack must match the active suit; in a chain, it must match the last card’s suit unless that card was a Jack. With 3+ players, a Jack ends the chain and skips the next player, after any 8 reversal. Otherwise, finish the chain with same-suit support (an 8 or Jack extends it) or draw 1. In a 2-player game, a Jack needs same-suit support; a Jack can extend another Jack but cannot be support. A support 2 also gives the next player a 2-card penalty.
  • KKing — A normal card; it has no special effect.
  • 7Seven — A normal card; it has no special effect.

Each turn has a 60-second timer. If time runs out, you draw the active penalty, or 1 card if there is none; your turn then ends. First player to empty their hand wins!
