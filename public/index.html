    THE QUICK VERSION

    How to play

    Each player starts with 4 cards. The opening card is never an Ace, 2, 8, Jack, or Joker. On your turn, play a card matching the top card by suit or number. If you cannot or do not want to play, draw 1; your turn ends, and you cannot play the card you drew.

    Suits: ♠ Spades, ♥ Hearts, ♣ Clubs, ♦ Diamonds.

    • AAce — “I want” — Play on the same suit or another Ace, then name a suit. The next player must play that suit or an Ace. If they cannot, they draw 1 and the next player must answer the demand. A Joker clears the demand.
    • 2Two — Pick 2 — Play on the same suit or another 2. The next player draws 2 and misses their turn, unless they answer with another 2, which cancels the penalty.
    • ✳Joker — Pick 4 — Play on anything except a 2. The next player draws 4 and misses their turn; the following player may play any card, regardless of the previous suit. They can instead answer with a Joker (cancels the penalty; the following player may play anything) or a 2 (blocks 2, so they draw 2 and the next player follows that 2’s suit). A Joker also clears an Ace’s demand.
    • JJack — Jump — Play on the same suit or another Jack. In group play, the next player is skipped. In 1v1 and solo play, the jump comes back to you: play a card in the Jack’s suit or draw 1. Another same-suit Jack can continue the chain.
    • 8Eight — Needs support — Play again straight away with a card of the 8’s suit, or another 8 (which needs its own support). If you have no support, draw 1. Completing the support turns play around. A Jack can support an 8; in 1v1 or solo, continue with a card matching the Jack’s suit, while in group play the Jack skips the next player.

    You cannot go out on a Jack, Ace, 8, 2, or Joker; if it is your last card, you must draw 1 on your next turn. When you have one card, you are automatically marked as knocked. In 1v1, the first player out wins. In group play, the last player holding cards each round is eliminated; play new rounds until one player remains.

    Each turn has a 60-second timer. If time runs out, draw the active penalty, or 1 card if there is none; your turn then ends.
