THE QUICK VERSION

How to play

Each player starts with 4 cards. The opening card is never an Ace, 2, 8, Jack, or Joker. On your turn, play a card matching the top card by suit or number. If you cannot or do not want to play, draw 1; your turn ends, and you cannot play the card you drew.

Suits: ♠ Spades, ♥ Hearts, ♣ Clubs, ♦ Diamonds.

  • AAce — “I want” — Play on the same suit or another Ace, then name a suit. The next player must play that suit or an Ace. If they cannot, they draw 1 and the next player must answer the demand. A Joker clears the demand.
  • 2Two — Pick 2 — Play on the same suit or another 2. The next player draws 2 and misses their turn, unless they answer with another 2, which cancels the penalty.
  • ✳Joker — Pick 4 — Play on anything except a 2. The next player draws 4 and misses their turn, unless they answer with a Joker (cancels it; the following player may play anything) or a 2 (blocks 2, so they draw 2). A Joker also clears an Ace’s demand.
  • JJack — Jump — Play on the same suit or another Jack. The next player is skipped. In a 2-player game, the jump comes back to you: play a normal support card or draw 1.
  • 8Eight — Needs support — Play again straight away with a card of the 8’s suit, or another 8 (which needs its own support). If you have no support, draw 1. Completing the support turns play around.

You cannot go out on a Jack, Ace, 8, 2, or Joker; if it is your last card, you must draw 1 on your next turn. Knock when you have one card; it does not use your turn. In 1v1, the first player out wins. In group play, the last player holding cards each round is eliminated; play new rounds until one player remains.

Each turn has a 60-second timer. If time runs out, draw the active penalty, or 1 card if there is none; your turn then ends.
